# Risk assessment process

**Draft for adoption — 2026-09-24.** Maintain an evidence-backed risk register; a source scanner score is not a risk assessment.

For every risk record asset/data, threat, vulnerability, likelihood, impact, existing control evidence, treatment, owner, deadline and residual acceptance. Consider clinical harm, privacy, integrity, availability and business continuity together. Use a documented likelihood/impact scale and prioritize high-impact uncertainty rather than inventing precise probabilities.

Initial areas: clinical authorization/schema blockers, key recovery, vendor failure, public claims, third-party media rights, dependency compromise, privileged access, backup loss, single failure domain and overload. [Threat model](../THREAT-MODEL.md) and [clinical gates](../CLINICAL-RELEASE.md) provide the current engineering starting point.

Proposed review cadence: before clinical launch, quarterly, after incidents and before material architecture/vendor/jurisdiction changes. Risk acceptance requires accountable leadership and an expiry; it does not waive law or patient-safety obligations. Preserve evidence and historical decisions privately.
