# Security awareness and role training

**Draft for adoption — 2026-09-24.** No completed workforce training program is asserted.

Require training before access to clinical systems, with proposed annual refreshers and targeted updates after incidents or major changes. Cover phishing, MFA, approved devices, minimum necessary access, patient identity/guardian checks, record confidentiality, secure messaging, rights requests, incident reporting and downtime handling.

| Role | Additional exercise |
|---|---|
| Reception | Scope-limited scheduling and identity verification |
| Clinician | Care assignment, corrections and emergency-access justification |
| Administrator | Role changes, revocation and audit review |
| Engineer/operator | Secrets, secure changes, restore and incident evidence |

Use synthetic scenarios only. Assess understanding through practical exercises, record attendance/results privately and remediate gaps before granting privileged access. Assign a training owner and review relevance to US/EU/Bangladesh duties. Training complements technical controls; it does not replace authorization tests.
