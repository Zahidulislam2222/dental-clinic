# BAA applicability and execution checklist

**Draft — 2026-09-24. No signed agreements asserted.**

| Review item | Evidence required |
|---|---|
| Parties/roles | Actual legal entities and covered-entity/business-associate determination |
| Service/data | What PHI is created, received, maintained or transmitted, including metadata/context |
| Provider eligibility | Exact service/plan/region permits required processing; current agreement available |
| Uses/disclosures | Defined permitted purposes and restrictions |
| Safeguards | Applicable Security Rule obligations, risk allocation and actual controls |
| Reporting | Incident/breach process, contacts, content and negotiated timelines consistent with law |
| Subcontractors | Appropriate downstream restrictions and responsibilities |
| Patient rights | Access, amendment and accounting assistance process |
| Oversight | Required access for HHS and contractual evidence |
| Termination | Material breach remedies, return/destruction and retained-data protections |
| Execution | Authorized signatures, dates, scope and renewal/review owner |

Do not automatically exempt CDN, email or support vendors: assess actual data flow and whether context reveals health information. No production PHI use until the applicable contract is executed and controls pass. A BAA alone does not make an insecure application suitable.

Review at onboarding, renewal and material change. Keep contracts, legal entities' private details and signatures out of Git. Refer to [HHS provisions](https://www.hhs.gov/hipaa/for-professionals/covered-entities/sample-business-associate-agreement-provisions/index.html) and [vendor policy](../policies/VENDOR_MANAGEMENT.md).
